How Customers Order Regasco LPG Online

Learn how to select cylinders, choose Refill vs New Tank, pinpoint delivery GPS locations, pay via COD or GCash, and track riders on the live map.

1

Select Cylinder & Option

Customers can browse the storefront and choose their required tank size:

  • 11 KG Regular (Most Popular): Standard household size for kitchens and home stoves.
  • 2.7 KG Camping / Small Stove: Compact and portable tank.
  • 50 KG Commercial / Industrial: For restaurants and bakeries.
  • Refill vs. New Tank:
    • Refill: Swap your empty cylinder for a newly refilled certified Regasco cylinder.
    • New Complete Tank: Includes brand new cylinder tank + valve seal.
2

Pinpoint Delivery Location

Accurate delivery pins ensure riders navigate straight to the gate:

  • "Use My Current Location" Button: Tapping this automatically acquires device GPS coordinates.
  • Interactive Map Pin: Drag the orange pin directly onto your rooftop or house gate.
  • Address & Landmark: Fill in street name, house number, and clear landmarks (e.g. "Beside 7-Eleven, Green Gate").
3

Choose Payment Method

Regasco offers two flexible payment channels:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Pay cash directly to the rider after the cylinder is delivered and leak-tested.
  • GCash Online: Scan the official store GCash QR code, enter the 13-digit Reference Number, and upload the payment receipt screenshot.
4

Live Order & GPS Tracking

After clicking Place Order, customers receive a unique Order Code (e.g., REG-8921):

  • Status Pipeline: Watch the order move from Pending → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered.
  • Live Radar: View the rider's motorcycle moving along the street in real-time.
  • 1-Tap Call: Call the assigned rider directly from the tracking page.
Customer Safety Standard Guarantee
Regasco delivery riders always inspect your hose and perform a complimentary soap bubble leak-test upon connecting your LPG cylinder.
Role: Store Manager & Dispatcher

Admin Hub & Central Dispatch Radar

Control live orders, dispatch fleet riders, manage product prices, adjust depot coordinates, and oversee delivery telemetry.

1

Admin Access & Login

Access the control center at /admin:

  • Enter your authorized Admin Username and Password.
  • Once verified, the session stays active in your browser.
  • To change admin credentials, visit the Store & Map APIs tab in the sidebar.
2

Managing Live Orders

The Orders table organizes transactions by lifecycle:

  • Pending: Newly placed customer orders awaiting store confirmation.
  • Assigning Rider: Click "Assign Rider" dropdown to pick an available rider on duty.
  • Print Receipt: Generate clean 80mm thermal receipt slips for the rider delivery pouch.
  • GCash Verification: Click to view customer receipt screenshots before approving.
3

Fleet & Rider Management

Under the Riders sidebar tab:

  • Add New Rider: Enter name, phone number, motorcycle plate number, and assign a 4-digit security PIN.
  • Status Overview: Monitor who is AVAILABLE, ON_DELIVERY, or OFFLINE.
  • Deactivate / Edit: Update rider details or remove inactive staff.
4

Depot GPS & Map Configuration

Under Store & Map APIs:

  • Depot Latitude & Longitude: Adjust your physical central depot location (e.g. Mindanao Ave Central Depot).
  • Store Open / Closed Switch: Toggle ordering availability in one click (e.g. during severe typhoons or holidays).
  • Delivery Fee & Free Threshold: Set standard delivery rates (e.g., ₱50 delivery, free above ₱2,000).

Rider Cockpit & Field Delivery Guide

Designed for motorcycle couriers: receiving instant dispatch alerts, road navigation, GPS telemetry broadcasting, and doorstep Proof of Delivery.

1

Logging in to the Cockpit

Open /rider on your Android smartphone or open the installed Regasco APK:

  • Enter your registered phone number (e.g. 0917-555-1201).
  • Enter your 4-digit security PIN.
  • Tap "LOG IN TO COCKPIT". Your session will remain saved on your phone.
  • Toggle your status to "On Duty" so the central depot can assign deliveries to you.
2

Receiving Incoming Orders

When the Central Hub assigns a new delivery mission:

  • Loud Audio Siren: A 3-tone siren plays from the phone speaker.
  • Vibration Buzz: The phone vibrates in your pocket or handlebar mount.
  • Action: Review customer address, cylinder quantity, and tap "Accept & Deliver".
3

Navigation & Calling Customer

While on the road to the destination:

  • Embedded Map: Review customer doorstep pin and route line.
  • "Open in Google Maps" Button: Tapping this opens the Google Maps app for hands-free voice turn-by-turn spoken guidance.
  • 1-Tap "Call Customer": Direct phone dialer if you need to ask for gate directions or buzz the doorbell.
4

Proof of Delivery (POD)

When you reach the customer’s house:

  • Tap "Arrived • Snap Proof of Delivery".
  • Camera Snap: Take a photo of the newly connected cylinder at the doorstep or kitchen stove.
  • Collect COD: If Cash on Delivery, check the collected amount checkbox.
  • Safety Inspection: Verify safety regulator valve is securely connected.
  • Tap "Confirm Delivery Complete". Confetti will celebrate completion!
Rider Phone Setting Requirement
Make sure your smartphone has Location / GPS enabled and that you tap "Allow" when your browser prompts for location access. Keep your phone mounted on your motorcycle handlebar with the screen active for smooth GPS tracking.

Maps, GPS & Technical Operations FAQ

Clear answers regarding map billing, GPS permissions, mobile APK installation, and live hosting questions.

Do I need to pay for the Map or GPS services on live deployment?
No, you do NOT need to pay anything. The entire map infrastructure is built on 100% free, open-source services:
• Visual Maps: OpenStreetMap (OSM) community tiles via Leaflet.js ($0).
• Road Geometry & Distance: OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine) ($0).
• Turn-by-Turn Rider Voice Guidance: Native Google Maps app deep-links ($0, no Google Cloud billing required).
• Customer GPS: Device hardware GPS via standard W3C Geolocation API ($0).
Why does the GPS location button require HTTPS (SSL) when live?
All modern web browsers (Chrome, Safari, iOS, Android) strictly block location access on unencrypted http:// websites to protect user privacy. When deployed to Vercel or any modern cloud hosting, free SSL (https://) is enabled automatically, so location permissions work without issues.
How do riders install the app on their Android phones?
Riders have two easy options:
Option A (Pre-packaged APK): Download and install /regasco-app.apk directly from the website onto any Android device.
Option B (PWA / Browser Shortcut): Open https://your-domain/rider in Google Chrome on mobile, tap the 3 dots menu → tap "Add to Home screen". This creates an app icon on their phone that opens in full-screen standalone mode.
What environment variables must be configured on Vercel?
For full multi-device live synchronization, configure:
DATABASE_URL = Your Neon PostgreSQL connection string (e.g. postgresql://neondb_owner:...@ep-....neon.tech/neondb?sslmode=require).
This guarantees that orders, rider GPS pings, and customer updates are stored in the cloud database and instantly shared across all active phones and computers.
How does GCash payment verification work?
When a customer selects GCash:
1. They send payment to the merchant's official GCash number/QR code displayed on the checkout screen.
2. They enter their 13-digit Reference Number and attach a screenshot receipt.
3. In the Admin Dashboard, the order displays a green GCash icon. The store manager clicks the attachment to inspect the receipt image before dispatching the rider.
